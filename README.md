
#  Myntra E-Commerce Analysis Dashboard

**Myntra E-Commerce Analysis Dashboard** is a data visualization project built using **Power BI**. The primary objective of this project is to analyze 2 years of e-commerce data (user interactions, product pricing, ratings, and customer engagement) to uncover critical **Data-Driven Insights** for business growth.

---

##  Data Source & Processing
* **Data Source:** The raw dataset for this project was sourced from **Kaggle**, representing realistic customer behavior on an e-commerce platform.
* **Data Cleaning & ETL:** Handled data cleaning, missing value treatments, and duplicate row removals using **Power Query** in Power BI to ensure data integrity.

---

##  Dashboard Structure (Pages)

The dashboard is structured into 4 main pages:
* **Front Page / Overview:** Displays core KPIs such as Total Users (**100K**), Average Rating (**3.75**), and customer interactions segmented by event types.
* **Product Pricing & Rating Insights:** Features price distribution curves, top-clicked items, and sales patterns analyzed against product ratings.
* **User Engagement & Interaction:** Tracks daily active hours (DateTime analysis) and identifies top-performing categories driving **7M Total Interactions**.
* **Insights Page:** Summarizes the 5 most critical business findings deduced from the data analysis.

---

##  Key Business Insights

1. **Year-over-Year Interaction Shift:** Over the last 2 years, total interactions reached 7M. While browsing and clicks increased rapidly, actual purchases did not grow at the same rate.
2. **Evolving Price Sensitivity:** Customer click patterns show a visible shift toward mid-to-low-priced products over the last 2 years, indicating that Myntra customers are becoming more price-conscious.
3. **Catalog Expansion vs Quality Assurance:** Although the volume of new products expanded significantly, the average rating remains stagnant at 3.75. To sustain platform trust, Myntra should filter out or remove low-rated sellers.
4. **Customer Preference Shifts:** Customer buying patterns have evolved over time. While certain fashion sub-categories dominated initially, fashion sub-categories recorded the highest engagement in the second year.
5. **Seasonality & Peak Activity Pattern:** Customer activity surges drastically during specific months and festival seasons. Myntra can leverage these peak periods to plan targeted marketing campaigns and manage inventory in advance.
